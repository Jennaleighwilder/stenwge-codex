Level 1 · The Guessing Machine

AI guesses the next word. That's the whole trick.

🎯 The idea

When AI writes, it's predicting the most likely next word — over and over — based on all the text it read. Like your phone's autocomplete, but supercharged. It doesn't look anything up. It just guesses what usually comes next.

Level 2 · How AI Learned

Nobody typed in the answers.
It learned from examples.

🎯 The idea

AI learned by reading a mountain of text and practicing. This is called training: it guesses the next word, checks how wrong it was, adjusts a little, and repeats — billions of times. The more it practices, the better it guesses. No rules were written by hand; the patterns just stuck.

Level 3 · The Confident Liar

AI can be confidently wrong.

🎯 The idea

AI is built to sound right, not to BE right. It has no fact-checker inside and never says "I don't know." So when it doesn't know, it makes something up that sounds perfect. This is called a hallucination — a confident answer that's just… false. She read the whole internet and will still make up a fake source with a straight face.

▶ Your turn: spot the lie

For each answer, decide: is the AI telling the truth or making it up? Tap to reveal — and see why.
